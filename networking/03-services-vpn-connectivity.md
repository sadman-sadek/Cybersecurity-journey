# Network Services, VPNs & Connectivity

This file focuses on naming services, secure communication protocols, and physical internet connection types.

---

## 1. DNS (Domain Name System)
* **DNS Servers:** 
  * Often called the "Phonebook of the Internet." They translate human-readable domain names (like `google.com`) into computer-readable IP addresses (like `142.250.190.46`).

---

## 2. Types of VPN (Virtual Private Network)
VPNs create an encrypted tunnel over the public internet to secure data transmission.

* **IPsec (Internet Protocol Security):** 
  * Operates at the **Network Layer (Layer 3)**. It encrypts the entire IP packet and is commonly used for secure Site-to-Site VPN connections between office branches.
* **SSL/TLS (Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security):** 
  * Operates at the **Transport/Application Layer**. It is commonly used for Remote Access VPNs (Client-to-Site), allowing users to connect securely via web browsers or specific lightweight client apps.

---

## 3. xDSL Technologies
Digital Subscriber Line (DSL) technologies provide internet access over traditional copper telephone lines.

* **ADSL (Asymmetric DSL):** 
  * Download speeds are much faster than upload speeds. Ideal for standard home users who download more than they upload.
* **SDSL (Symmetric DSL):** 
  * Download and upload speeds are exactly equal. Ideal for businesses hosting servers or uploading large amounts of data.
* **VDSL (Very-high-bit-rate DSL):** 
  * A much faster version of DSL that works over short copper line distances, often acting as the final connection leg in Fiber-to-the-Cabinet (FTTC) setups.
