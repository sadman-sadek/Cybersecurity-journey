# Physical Networking

## Copper

Electrical signals travel through copper.

Common Ethernet twisted-pair categories include different performance levels and maximum channel specifications.

## Fiber

Light travels through optical fiber.

Broad categories:

- single-mode
- multimode

Fiber is useful for long distances and high bandwidth and is resistant to electromagnetic interference.

## Wireless

Radio waves carry information through the air.

Wireless introduces variables such as:

- interference
- channel contention
- signal strength
- attenuation
- obstacles
- channel width

## Physical-layer mindset

When a link fails:

1. Is the interface physically connected?
2. Is there link/carrier?
3. Is the negotiated speed/duplex sensible?
4. Is the medium damaged?
5. Is the correct transceiver/cable type used?

Do not jump directly to IP troubleshooting.
