## Concepts

Learn:

- SSID
- BSSID
- channels
- 2.4/5/6 GHz concepts
- association
- authentication
- encryption
- roaming
- management frames
- WPA2/WPA3 concepts

## Security

Understand:

- weak credentials
- rogue APs
- evil-twin concepts
- deauthentication abuse concept
- insecure guest networks
- poor segmentation
- management interface exposure

Only test wireless attacks in authorized environments.

## Defensive questions

- Is guest traffic isolated?
- Is management traffic separated?
- Is enterprise authentication used where appropriate?
- Are legacy protocols disabled?
- Are unknown APs monitored?
